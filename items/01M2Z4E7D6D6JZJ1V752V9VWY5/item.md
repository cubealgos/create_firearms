## Scope

Kevin, 2026-09-20, on FA-21's round-one sheet: "they should look more like Create / vanilla Minecraft items, they currently don't have that vibe at all + they should be 3D models I suppose." Recorded as `decisions/DEC-018-art-direction.md`, `WEAPON-REQ-016` and `WEAPON-REQ-017`. This ticket is the 3D half: every base weapon becomes a cuboid element model with its own texture atlas, every mounted attachment a cuboid part model at its slot's anchor, composited as before (`ARCH-DEC-006`) and rendered as a 3D model in every display context, the way Create renders its potato cannon. Generated deterministically by a new `tools/models.py` from a box list per weapon and per part, textured from `tools/palette.py`. FA-21 (round two, in parallel) redraws the flat icons for standalone attachments and cartridges; this ticket does not touch those.

## Approach

`tools/models.py`: a box DSL (from/to in 16ths, material ramp, optional face overrides), an atlas packer that gives every box face its own pixels (light tone on up/left faces, base on north/south, shade on down/right, outline pixels along every face edge), per-weapon `models/item/weapon/<id>.json` with `texture_size`, `elements` and `display` transforms per class started from Create's potato cannon, per-part `models/item/weapon/part/<slot>_<name>.json` positioned per class at model-unit anchors, and `items/weapon.json` regenerated as the same composite/condition/select tree over the cuboid models. Render a sheet per round from the model definitions (an orthographic preview drawn from the box list, plus the atlases) to the scratchpad for Kevin. Up to three rounds.
